Privacy Policy for Localité

Effective Date: May 23, 2026

Localité collects information needed to provide real-time social coordination, including account information, profile information, selected activities, group participation, and location information when permitted by the user.

Location Information

Localité may collect precise location information during active sharing sessions. Location sharing is controlled by the user and is based on selected groups, friends, activities, and session settings. Users can stop sharing by ending a session or changing device permissions.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate the app, enable location-based discovery, support friend and group coordination, improve safety, send notifications, maintain security, and improve the user experience.

Sharing Information

Information may be shared with other users according to your visibility settings. We may also use service providers for hosting, authentication, notifications, analytics, and app infrastructure.

Data Retention

We retain information as needed to provide the service, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, and improve app functionality. Some session and location data may be deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

User Controls

Users can update profile information, manage visibility settings, disable location permissions, disable notifications, or request account deletion.

Children’s Privacy

Localité is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Localité also maintains separate Child Safety Standards covering child sexual abuse and exploitation, CSAM, reporting, and enforcement.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect user information, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@localite-app.com.
